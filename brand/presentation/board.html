What we heard

The brief

A council of AI analysts reads a company's annual report, earnings call and news, debates the bull and bear case, and writes a balanced, fully cited briefing. It informs and never advises (SEBI-safe): no buy/sell calls, every claim traced to its source.

rigorousbalancedtransparentIndian

Success: says 'every claim is cited' at a glance, avoids trading clichés (bulls, bears, candlesticks, arrows, the rupee sign), reads at 16 px, works in one colour.

Final design

Cited

Folded citation brackets hold three lines of a source; the highlighted line is the exact quote behind a claim.

  • Says the product promise, 'nothing without a source', without drawing a chart or an animal
  • Neel and Haldi: two Indian dye colours; indigo for trust, turmeric for the highlighted quote
  • The haldi highlight repeats as the dot of the i in the custom wordmark and on every verified quote in the app
