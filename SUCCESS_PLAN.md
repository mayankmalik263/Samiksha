## 🧰 Build-in-public playbook

**Cadence:** 1 post/day minimum on **X** (fastest, dev-friendly) + **LinkedIn** (highest-quality audience for fintech). **Instagram** 3–4 times (Days 3, 4, 6, 10) with reels/carousels — it's visual, so save it for screen-recordings and diagrams.

**Each post should have:**
- A **hook** in the first line (a question, a bold claim, or a number).
- One **visual** (screenshot, screen-recording, diagram, or the briefing output).
- A **"Day N / 10"** tag so people feel the countdown.
- A soft **CTA** ("follow for the next update", "what stock should I test it on?").

**What to capture as you build** (screenshot/record these — you'll reuse them):
- The architecture diagram.
- Agents producing cited output in the terminal.
- The live "council debate" streaming in the UI.
- A full briefing for a famous stock.
- Any funny bug / "aha" moment.
